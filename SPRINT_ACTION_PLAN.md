2. Assess team velocity required to complete
3. Identify which items are highest priority

#### Action Item 9: Team Training
**Timeline:** Next sprint  
**Owner:** Product Owner / Scrum Master  
**Duration:** 1 hour training session

**Topics:**
1. **Why estimates matter:**
   - Without estimates, we can't plan
   - We can't commit to delivery dates
   - We can't assess risk
   - We can't allocate resources

2. **How to estimate:**
   - Planning poker technique
   - Relative sizing
   - Identifying unknowns
   - Breaking down complex items

3. **Story point anchors:**
   - "This is our 1-point reference story"
   - "2-point items are 2x more complex than 1-point"
   - Etc.

4. **When to re-estimate:**
   - When new information emerges
   - When work starts and surprises occur
   - Never mid-sprint (original estimate stands)
